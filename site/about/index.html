The story behind Sea More

Look closer.

The more we understand the natural world, the more extraordinary it becomes.

Where it began

A small shark. A much bigger question.

Sea More's story begins with a young diver who was wary of sharks. During a first dive course, a small reef shark appeared near the coral. Curiosity replaced fear, and the animal swam away.

That moment started a search to understand these creatures through experience. Sea More was created to offer others the same chance to observe, learn and return with deeper respect.

How we travel

The right place. The right people.

We focus on locations chosen for the wildlife encounter, and work with experienced professionals to help guests make the most of every journey. The wild remains wild; that is part of its wonder.
